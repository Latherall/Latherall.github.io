About Roadside Refinish

We're a mobile SMART repair specialist focusing on small area repairs and alloy wheel refurbishments. Rather than spending your day waiting at a bodyshop, we come to you – whether that's at home, at work, or wherever suits you best.

From scuffed alloys and bumper scrapes to minor paint damage, we handle the everyday knocks and scratches that come with life on the road. Our mobile setup means professional results without the inconvenience, and often at a fraction of traditional bodyshop prices.

Our Services

Professional mobile repairs for all common cosmetic damage

Alloy Wheel Refurbishment

Kerbed, scuffed or scratched alloys restored to showroom condition. Mobile service available for most wheel types and finishes.

Bumper Scuffs

Car park scrapes, low wall damage and minor bumper scuffs repaired on-site. Colour matched and blended for an invisible repair.

Paintwork Repairs

Stone chips, scratches and small paint defects professionally repaired. Precision colour matching for a seamless finish.

Minor Bodywork Repairs

Small dents, dings and cosmetic damage repaired without the need for expensive panel replacement. Quick, convenient and cost-effective.
